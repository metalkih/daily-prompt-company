Privacy Policy for Apps with Advertising

This privacy policy applies to mobile applications published by Daily Prompt Company and created by Inhong Kim (the "Service Provider") when the application's App Store listing links to this page. Some applications operate primarily with data stored on your device or public data retrieved from official sources. For example, EV incentive apps may request public vehicle incentive and regional status information from official government websites and display it in a mobile-friendly format.

Applications covered by this policy may also include third-party services for app analytics, diagnostics, and advertising, including Google Firebase and Google AdMob. These services may collect or receive information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising or app-scoped identifiers
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • App interactions, ad views, ad clicks, video views, and similar usage data
  • Crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostics
  • Advertising data, such as information about ads shown in the app

The Service Provider does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information through these applications.

Information may be used for the following purposes:
